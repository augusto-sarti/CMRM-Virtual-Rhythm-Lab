# Provenance and attribution

## Bruno Di Giorgi
Original rhythm-analysis software, phase-autocorrelation implementations, rhythmogram/tempo/beat-tracking line, and synthetic rhythmic examples including *Sound of Muzak*. Developed during PhD work at Politecnico di Milano under Augusto Sarti. Permission to reuse and modernize has been obtained by Augusto Sarti.

## Riccardo Gianpiccolo
Original APM notebook and pedagogical decomposition of time-instant influence in the lag/phase plane.

## CMRM teaching reconstruction
The notebooks in `notebooks/` reorganize, normalize notation, modernize implementation, and add new controlled experiments, including the cyclostationary/polyphase interpretation, synthetic onset examples, tempo-path visualization, beat-phase ambiguity, and algebraic cluster transformations.
