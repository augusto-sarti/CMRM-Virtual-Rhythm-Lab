# Provenance and attribution

This document distinguishes original source material from the later CMRM teaching reconstruction.

## Bruno Di Giorgi

Original contributions include rhythm-analysis software, phase-autocorrelation implementations, the rhythmogram/tempo/beat-tracking line, and synthetic rhythmic examples including *Sound of Muzak*. These materials were developed during Bruno Di Giorgi's PhD work at Politecnico di Milano under Augusto Sarti.

Permission to reuse and modernize this material for the CMRM teaching project has been obtained by Augusto Sarti.

### Synthetic Sound of Muzak WAV

`assets/sound_of_muzak_synthetic.wav` is the synthetic reference file developed in Bruno Di Giorgi's original teaching/research material. It is included with permission for the CMRM teaching project.

The commercial Porcupine Tree recording is not included in this repository.

## Riccardo Gianpiccolo

The APM material incorporates the original notebook and pedagogical decomposition of time-instant influence in the lag/phase plane developed by Riccardo Gianpiccolo.

## CMRM teaching reconstruction

The notebooks in `notebooks/` reorganize the source material into a progressive teaching sequence, normalize notation, modernize implementation, and add new explanatory and experimental material. Additions include controlled onset examples, the cyclostationary/polyphase interpretation, tempo-path visualization, beat-phase ambiguity experiments, algebraic rhythmic-cluster transformations, and an end-to-end representation study built around the *Sound of Muzak* synthetic example.

Where legacy algorithms or examples are reused or adapted, the notebooks retain explicit attribution. The pedagogical organization, explanatory text, integration across representations, and modernized notebook implementations form part of the CMRM teaching reconstruction.
